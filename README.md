# Pepito Manaloto

My favorite Filipino sitcom is **Pepito Manaloto**, which follows the hilarious and heartwarming life of Pepito, a humble guy who suddenly wins the lottery and navigates life as a newly rich family man while staying grounded.

## Why It's a Classic
* **Relatable Humor:** Mixes everyday Filipino family dynamics, neighborhood antics, and workplace comedy.
* **Memorable Characters:** Pepito, Elsa, Chito, Clarissa, Patrick, and the household staff (Maria, Robert, Baby) make every episode entertaining.
* **Heart & Moral Lessons:** Beyond the comedy, every episode leaves a genuine lesson about family, friendship, humility, and smart financial choices.
